I provide research and strategy consulting to help organizations translate data and stakeholder perspectives into reports, guidance, and strategic direction.

Areas of support

I work with nonprofits, research organizations, think tanks, economic development organizations, and mission-driven companies that are working through a complex decision or question.

Strategic decision-making

I help organizations gather information, analyze options, communicate results to key stakeholders, and determine a path forward.

An engagement may include

  • Identifying meaningful indicators
  • Developing decision frameworks
  • Analyzing relevant datasets
  • Interviewing stakeholders, experts, and potential partners
  • Mapping opportunities and risks
  • Recommending next steps

Potential deliverables

  • Quantitative scoring models
  • Syntheses of findings
  • Opportunity assessments
  • Strategic recommendations
  • Memos and board presentations

Research & policy materials

I help organizations investigate challenging questions and develop clear, evidence-based outputs for policy or professional audiences.

An engagement may include

  • Reviewing and synthesizing existing research
  • Gathering input from experts and stakeholders
  • Distilling themes from research findings
  • Developing guidance or recommendations
  • Structuring and drafting substantive written materials
  • Communicating findings to different audiences

Potential deliverables

  • Research reports
  • Policy briefs and recommendations
  • Frameworks or guidance documents
  • Case studies
  • Presentations and supporting materials
