Privacy notice

This notice explains how information is collected and used when you visit kathrynrbrink.com or submit a project inquiry.

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Information you provide

If you use the contact form, you provide your name, email address, the name of your organization if you choose to include it, and the contents of your message. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through the form.

Information collected automatically

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit a GitHub Pages site, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes and may collect other technical information as described in the GitHub General Privacy Statement ↗.

When you submit the contact form, the submission is processed by Formspree. Formspree may collect technical information such as your IP address, browser type, access time, and referring website address in addition to the information you enter. More information is available in the Formspree Privacy Policy ↗.

How information is used

Information submitted through the contact form is used to review and respond to your inquiry, communicate with you about a potential or ongoing engagement, maintain appropriate business records, prevent spam and misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. Contact-form information is not used to add you to a marketing list without your separate consent.

Service providers and disclosure

GitHub provides website hosting, and Formspree processes and stores contact-form submissions and sends submission notifications. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect legal rights, or address fraud, security, or safety concerns.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not include first-party analytics, advertising pixels, or marketing cookies. GitHub and Formspree may use logs, cookies, or similar technologies when providing their services, as described in their respective privacy policies.

Retention

Contact-form information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, manage professional relationships, maintain appropriate business records, and satisfy legal obligations. Formspree stores submissions within the associated Formspree account.

Your choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including requesting access, correction, or deletion. To make a request, use the contact form and indicate that your message concerns privacy.

International processing

GitHub and Formspree may process information in the United States and other countries. Privacy protections in those locations may differ from those where you live.

Children’s privacy

This website is intended for professional audiences and is not directed to children under 13. Personal information from children under 13 is not knowingly collected.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated as the website or its service providers change. The date at the top of the notice shows when it was last revised.

Contact

For questions or requests concerning this notice, please use the contact form.
